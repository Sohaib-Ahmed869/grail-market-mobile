# Grading company logos

Drop the official logo here with the exact filename below, then add its line to
`lib/graderlogos.ts`. The chip switches from the drawn wordmark to the real
logo with no other change.

    psa.png     Professional Sports Authenticator
    bgs.png     Beckett Grading Services
    cgc.png     Certified Guaranty Company
    tag.png     TAG Grading
    sgc.png     Sportscard Guaranty
    ace.png     ACE Grading
    ags.png     Automated Grading Systems

Wanted: PNG with a transparent background, at least 240px wide, the horizontal
wordmark rather than the square avatar. Every one of these companies publishes
a press or media kit; that is the copy to use.

These are registered trademarks belonging to their owners. They are used here
to identify whose grade is on a slab, which is what the marks are for. Do not
recolour, stretch, or place them on a background that alters them.
